Privacy Policy

Applies to the app “Weltgeschichte: Epochen & Quiz” (iOS, Android) and the web app. Last updated: 18 October 2026. This is a courtesy translation; the German version is legally binding.

In short

The app itself does not collect or transmit any personal data to us. There are no user accounts and no registration. All content is included in the app; it also works offline. The app is funded by advertising from Google (AdMob in the apps, AdSense in the web version). Google processes data as described below; personalised ads are only shown with your consent.

What the app stores locally

To remember bookmarks, quiz best scores, the last timeline filter, the chosen language and the colour scheme, the app stores these settings only on your device (in the local storage of the app or browser). This data never leaves the device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can delete it at any time in the app under “More” or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising in the apps (Google AdMob)

The iOS and Android apps show ads via Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): a banner at the bottom of the screen, occasionally a full-screen ad after a finished quiz or game round and – only if you start it yourself – a video for the 50:50 lifeline. AdMob may process device information, including advertising identifiers (Advertising ID or IDFA), IP address, device and connection data and approximate location, in order to deliver, personalise and measure ads and to prevent fraud.

Consent: On first launch the app uses Google’s consent dialog (User Messaging Platform) to ask whether you agree to personalised advertising and to access to your device. On iOS the app additionally asks via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT). Without consent you only see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under “More” → “Ad privacy”, and in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads).

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR in conjunction with § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021). More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Advertising in the web version (Google AdSense)

The web version may show an ad below the content via Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited, address as above). Google sets cookies or similar technologies and processes IP address, browser and device information and the page visited. A consent notice from Google asks for your agreement first; without it only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice under “More” → “Ad privacy”. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) TKG 2021).

Transfers to the USA and retention by Google

Google may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR); in addition, Google uses EU standard contractual clauses. How long Google keeps advertising data is governed by Google’s retention periods. We ourselves do not receive or store any advertising data about you, only aggregated reports (e.g. number of ads shown and revenue).

Hosting of the web version

Apart from advertising, the iOS and Android apps do not download content from the internet. The web version at almaz6380.github.io/Geschichte is delivered via GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA), a Microsoft Corporation company. As with any website visit, technically necessary connection data (IP address, time, requested page, user agent) briefly appears in the hosting provider’s server logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The transfer to the USA is based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which GitHub is certified (adequacy decision of the European Commission, Art. 45 GDPR). We do not analyse these logs ourselves.

No other services

Apart from Google advertising, no analytics or social media services are integrated. We do not pass any data on to third parties.

Children

The content is written for school and general education. The advertising is aimed at users aged 13 and over; the app is not specifically directed at children under 13. Without consent – which in Austria young people under 14 can only give with their parents’ approval – only non-personalised ads are shown.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at). Since we ourselves store no personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to disclose; for advertising data, please contact Google (see above). You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. You delete locally stored settings yourself in the app or by uninstalling it.
