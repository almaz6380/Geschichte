Privacy Policy

Applies to the app “Weltgeschichte: Epochen & Quiz” (iOS, Android) and the web app. Last updated: September 2026. This is a courtesy translation; the German version is legally binding.

Controller

Josef Gallab (sole proprietorship), Oberhofenweg 21b, 6380 St. Johann in Tirol, Austria, email: kontakt@wellbooked.at. Further details in the legal notice.

In short

The app itself does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There are no user accounts, no registration, no advertising and no tracking. All content is included in the app; it works completely offline.

What the app stores locally

To remember bookmarks, quiz best scores, the last timeline filter, the chosen language and the colour scheme, the app stores these settings only on your device (in the local storage of the app or browser). This data never leaves the device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can delete it at any time in the app under “More” or by uninstalling the app.

No connection to servers – with one exception: hosting of the web version

The native apps for iOS and Android do not download content from the internet and do not send any data. The web version at geschichte-gilt.vercel.app is delivered by Vercel Inc. (USA). As with any website visit, technically necessary connection data (IP address, time, requested page, user agent) briefly appears in the hosting provider’s server logs. This is the only processing of personal data in this service; the legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), safeguarded by a data processing agreement with EU standard contractual clauses. We do not analyse these logs ourselves.

No disclosure to third parties

No analytics, advertising or social media services are integrated. No data is passed on to third parties.

Children

The app is intended for all age groups and is suitable for children. Since no data is collected, no data from children is processed either.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at). Since we process no personal data apart from the hosting provider’s server logs, there is usually nothing for us to disclose. You delete locally stored settings yourself in the app or by uninstalling it.

Contact

Questions about data protection: kontakt@wellbooked.at. The app’s source code and change history are public: github.com/almaz6380/Geschichte.
