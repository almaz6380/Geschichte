Privacy Policy

Applies to the app “Weltgeschichte: Epochen & Quiz” (iOS, Android) and the web app. Last updated: September 2026 (version 1.1). This is a courtesy translation; the German version is legally binding.

Controller

Josef Gallab (sole proprietorship), Oberhofenweg 21b, 6380 St. Johann in Tirol, Austria, email: kontakt@wellbooked.at. Further details in the legal notice.

In short

The app has no user accounts and no registration, and we do not analyse your usage ourselves. All content is included in the app; it works offline. The iOS and Android apps show ads via Google AdMob. Google processes data from your device for this, as described below – personalised only with your consent. The web version contains no ads.

What the app stores locally

To remember bookmarks, quiz best scores, the last timeline filter, the chosen language and the colour scheme, the app stores these settings only on your device (in the local storage of the app or browser). This data never leaves the device and is not transmitted to us or to third parties. You can delete it at any time in the app under “More” → “Delete all data” or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising in the apps (Google AdMob)

The iOS and Android apps are funded by advertising: a banner at the bottom, occasionally a full-screen ad after a quiz and – voluntarily – an ad that gives you a 50:50 joker. The ads are delivered by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”) through its AdMob service.

Data processed: your device’s advertising ID (on iOS only if you allow tracking; on Android the advertising ID), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device information (model, operating system, language), information about the app, and your interactions with ads (e.g. impressions, clicks).

Purposes: serving and billing ads, measuring reach, preventing fraud and – only with your consent – personalising ads.

Consent and legal basis: On first launch, Google’s consent dialogue asks whether you agree to personalised advertising and to storing or accessing information on your device (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 165(3) Austrian TKG 2021). If you decline, Google only shows non-personalised ads where no consent is required (legitimate interest in funding the free app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). On iPhone, Apple additionally asks whether the app may track your activity across other apps; without your permission the advertising ID is not used.

Withdrawal: You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under “More” → “Ads and privacy”, and on iPhone also under Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

Transfers to the USA: Google may also process data in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses apply in addition.

More information: Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Hosting of the web version

The web version at geschichte-gilt.vercel.app is delivered by Vercel Inc. (USA). As with any website visit, technically necessary connection data (IP address, time, requested page, user agent) briefly appears in the hosting provider’s server logs. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure delivery (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), safeguarded by a data processing agreement with EU standard contractual clauses. We do not analyse these logs ourselves.

Disclosure to third parties

Apart from Google for advertising (see above) and the host of the web version, we do not pass on any data. No analytics or social media services are integrated.

Children

The app is intended for teenagers aged 13 and over and for adults.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at). We do not store any personal data about you ourselves; for advertising data, please contact Google directly (see above). You delete locally stored settings yourself in the app or by uninstalling it.
